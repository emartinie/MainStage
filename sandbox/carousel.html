Biblical Feasts- Here is a breakdown of the upcoming holidays:

Sukkot (Feast of Tabernacles/Booths):

Begins the evening of October 6, 2025, and ends the evening of October 13, 2025. This is a harvest festival where people build temporary "sukkah" booths to commemorate the Israelites' wandering in the wilderness.

Shemini Atzeret/Simchat Torah:

Follows Sukkot, beginning the evening of October 13, 2025. Shemini Atzeret marks the end of the holiday of Sukkot, while Simchat Torah celebrates the completion of the annual Torah reading cycle.

Hanukkah (Feast of Dedication):

This Biblical festival (mentioned in the New Testament and apocrypha) is celebrated in the winter usually around the pagan christmas holiday. For 2025, Hanukkah will begin the evening of December 14 and last until the evening of December 22, 2025.
