🕊️ Fulfillment in Messiah

  • Passover: Jesus’ death fulfills the Passover lamb.
  • Unleavened Bread: His burial during this feast symbolizes the sinless “bread of life.”
  • First Fruits: His resurrection as the firstfruits of those raised from the dead.
  • Pentecost: Spirit given 50 days later, new covenant sealed in His blood.
  • Trumpets: Often seen as a signal of His return.
  • Day of Atonement: Linked to final judgment and His coming as King.
  • Tabernacles: Picture of the 1,000-year Messianic kingdom.

📜 Spring & Fall Feasts

  • Pesach (Passover): Deliverance from Egypt; pointing to the Lamb.
  • Matzot (Unleavened Bread): Hasty departure; purity and humility.
  • Bikkurim (First Fruits): First harvest and resurrection picture.
  • Shavuot (Pentecost): Torah given; Spirit poured out.
  • Yom Teruah (Trumpets): New year; trumpet blast and awakening.
  • Yom Kippur (Day of Atonement): Fasting, repentance, covering of sin.
  • Sukkot (Tabernacles): Dwelling in booths; God with His people.
