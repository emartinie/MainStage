Bible Bingo — How It Works

  • B → Numbers 1–15
  • I → Numbers 16–30
  • B → Numbers 31–45
  • L → Numbers 46–60
  • E → Numbers 61–75
  • • Numbers are called randomly and never repeated.
  • • Players use their own Bingo cards (printed or handwritten).
  • • The app acts as the caller and scorekeeper only.
  • • No accounts, no syncing, no tracking.
  • • When you have Bingo, call it out clearly.

Designed for groups, gatherings, and shared spaces.

Scoreboard

The Vision (unchanged) Bible Bingo is communal, simple, and hosted. Not gamified chaos. Not kids-only. Not a dopamine slot machine. It’s: • One caller • Many people • Shared pace • Shared space • Scripture-adjacent, not distracting from Scripture That’s the non-negotiable. ⸻ What “Finished” Actually Means Not everything—just enough: 1. Caller is authoritative • Clear B / I / B / L / E callout • Visual display (big, calm, readable) • Spoken call (My son’s voice is perfect for this) If this works, Bingo works. 2. Pace feels human • Manual call ✅ • Auto-call (slow, optional) ✅ • Pause / resume (for conversation & prayer moments) Anything faster breaks the spirit of Bingo. ⸻ 3. Zero pressure on players • No accounts • No tracking • No cheating prevention • No “win logic” yet People bring their own cards. The app is the room, not the referee. ⸻ 4. A small, reverent Rules panel You already started this—and that matters. Rules do two things: • Set expectations • Calm anxiety They tell people: “You’re safe here. This is familiar.”
